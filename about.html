A spitbraai has a special place at a gathering. It gives people a reason to come together, take their time and enjoy the occasion.

Boland Spitbraai is a family-owned catering business that began in 2000 after a spitbraai for a friend’s 30th birthday. Through word of mouth, it grew into a recognised local service built around good food and looking after people.

Whether you're arranging a wedding, a family celebration or a work function, the aim is simple — make the food part of the memory.

“A great gathering begins with people — and a table worth sharing.”

ROOTED IN THE BOLAND

Based at 40 Jan van Riebeeck Drive in Paarl, the business serves customers within a published 150km radius, including the Cape Winelands and surrounding areas. The team caters for private home functions and corporate events at a venue of your choice.
