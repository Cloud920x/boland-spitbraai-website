A FAMILY-OWNED BUSINESS

Good food.
Generous portions.
Real hospitality.

Boland Spitbraai began in 2000 with a spitbraai for a friend’s 30th birthday. Word of mouth helped the family-owned business grow into a catering service for gatherings big and small.

Based in Paarl, the team serves events across the Boland, Cape Winelands and surrounding areas. Catering is available at your home or a venue of your choice.
